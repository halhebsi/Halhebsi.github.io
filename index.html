Alfred

Alfred is a private personal assistant built and used by one person for his own day-to-day organisation. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.

What it does with Google

With its owner's permission, Alfred reads his Google Calendar to answer questions such as "what's next today?", and creates or changes events only when he asks it to.
