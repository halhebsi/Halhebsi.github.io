Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Alfred is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

Alfred requests one Google permission: access to calendar events (calendar.events). It reads event titles, times, locations and attendees on its owner's own calendar, and creates or changes events only when the owner asks.

How the data is used

  • To answer the owner's own questions about his schedule and to remind him of upcoming events.
  • To create or update events at the owner's request.
  • To answer a request, relevant event details may be processed by the AI model providers Alfred uses to understand and reply to the owner, and the conversation may be kept in Alfred's private long-term memory service so it can remember context for him. These services receive only what is needed for that purpose.

Storage and security

The Google access token and any calendar data Alfred keeps are stored on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner's account, and reachable only through his private network.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone other than the processing described above. Alfred's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Alfred then stops reading the calendar, and its stored token is deleted on request.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Alfred's Google consent screen.
